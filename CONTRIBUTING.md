# Contributing to ZinTrust

Thank you for your interest in contributing to ZinTrust!

To help keep our community welcoming and maintain high code quality, please read our [Code of Conduct](.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and refer to the **[Contributor & QA Guide](docs/contributing.md)**.

## Quick Start

1. Read the [Contributor Guide](docs/contributing.md).
2. Find a `good-first-issue` in our [Issue Tracker](https://github.com/ZinTrust/ZinTrust/issues).
3. Follow the [Environment Setup](docs/contributing.md#environment-setup).
4. Run `zin qa` before submitting your Pull Request.

We look forward to your contributions!
